Privacy Policy

Effective date: 26 October 2026

Eldrin (“the app”) is published by ReibLAR LLC. This policy explains what the app and this website do with information. The short version: they collect none.

Information we collect

None. Eldrin has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store, sell or share any personal information, and the app contains no third-party SDKs that do.

What stays on your device

The app stores your saved game, your light or dark theme choice, and a note that you have unlocked the full game, on your device only. This information is never sent to us. It is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

If you buy the full game, the purchase is processed entirely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We do not receive your payment details or your name. The app only asks the store whether the purchase is valid. Apple’s and Google’s own privacy policies apply to the store.

This website

playeldrin.com is a static website. It sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as IP address and time of request) for security and operation.

Email

If you write to us, we use your message and address only to reply, and we do not share them.

Children

Eldrin is a general-audience game. It does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example, if analytics are ever added), we will update this page and the effective date.

Contact

Questions: support@playeldrin.com
