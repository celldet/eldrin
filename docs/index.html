Questions

More answers on the support page.

How much does it cost?

The first chapter is free. One one-time purchase unlocks the rest of the story. No ads, no subscription.

Do I need an account?

No. Eldrin has no accounts and collects no personal information.

Is there a hint system?

Yes. Type hint for a nudge or help to see the commands.

Where are my saves?

On your device only. Deleting the app deletes the saves.

I bought it on another phone.

Open the menu and choose Restore purchase, signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account.

Which devices?

iPhone and Android phones.
